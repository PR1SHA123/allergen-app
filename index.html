Before you start

This app helps you check food, but it is not medical advice.

  • It can make mistakes or miss ingredients. Labels change, photos can be misread, and food databases and AI can be wrong.
  • Always read the package label and ask the cook. If you're not sure, don't eat it.
  • Talk to your doctor or allergist about your allergies and what's safe for you.
  • In an emergency, use epinephrine if it was prescribed, and call 911 (or your local emergency number).

About, safety & privacy

Safety

This app is a helper, not medical advice, and it can be wrong. Always read the label, ask the cook, and follow your doctor's advice. In an emergency, use epinephrine if prescribed and call 911.

How it decides

The app checks ingredient lists against its own allergen dictionary (English, Hindi, Chinese, and Korean, including hidden names like whey and casein). Dishes come from a built-in guide of common recipes. Results marked ✨ AI estimate come from Google's Gemini AI and should be double-checked.

Your privacy

  • Your allergies, groups, and buddy are saved only on this phone. There are no accounts.
  • Share codes contain names and allergies, so only share them with people you trust.
  • Barcodes are looked up in Open Food Facts.
  • Food photos and the dish or allergy names you look up with ✨ AI are sent to Google's Gemini AI to answer you. Don't photograph people.
  • Label photos are read on your phone and aren't uploaded.
